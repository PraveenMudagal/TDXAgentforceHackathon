**Use Case Title:**
Leveraging Salesforce-AgentForce for Critical Illness Support in Healthcare

**Overview:**
Hospitals often collaborate with government schemes, NGOs, and charitable organizations to support patients dealing with critical illnesses. However, patients are often unaware of these opportunities, and hospital administrators face challenges managing multiple programs and stakeholders efficiently.

** Problem Statement**
	1. Patients with critical illnesses often lack awareness of available financial aid.
	2. Hospitals struggle to coordinate with NGOs, charities, and government schemes.
  	3. Manual tracking of schemes, donors, and patient eligibility is inefficient.
 
**Vision: Transforming Access to Aid with AgentForce**
	• Empower patients to discover funding support using AI-powered assistance.
	• Enable hospital administrators to manage partnerships and eligibility data efficiently.
	• Automate donor communications and case matching.

**Key Personas/Actors:**
	• Patient: Diagnosed with a critical illness and in need of financial aid.
	• Hospital Administrator: Manages support programs and assists patients in accessing aid.
	• Donors/NGOs/Charities: Organizations or individuals providing funds or support.

Persona --> Role --> Needs
Patient --> Seeks financial aid	Discover relevant schemes --> apply easily
Hospital Admin --> Coordinates aid	Track --> onboard partners --> manage cases
NGO/Donor --> Provides funding --> Get matched with genuine needs

**Key Capabilities of the Agent:**
	1. For Patients:
		○ Discover available government schemes, NGO initiatives, or charity programs tailored to their medical condition and financial situation.
		○ Check eligibility through guided conversations and automated data collection.
		○ Apply or get connected to available resources seamlessly through the agent interface.
	2. For Hospital Administrators:
		○ Maintain and update a centralized database of schemes, donors, NGOs, and their eligibility criteria.
		○ Onboard new support organizations and track interactions.
		○ View real-time insights on scheme utilization and patient engagement.
	3. For Donors/NGOs/Charities:
		○ Receive automated email alerts when a matching patient case arises based on pre-defined preferences (e.g., illness type, age, geography).
		○ Communicate directly with hospital staff or patients (if allowed) through secure messaging or contact workflows.

**Impact:**
	• Improved access to life-saving financial support for underserved patients.
	• Streamlined management of complex aid ecosystems by hospital staff.
	• Increased donor satisfaction through timely and relevant engagement.
